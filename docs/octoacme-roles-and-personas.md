# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. It covers both core delivery roles and supporting personas that contribute to project success, including Developers, Product Managers, Project Managers, Automation Engineers, Data Analysts, and Stakeholder Champions.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Automation Engineer

### Role Summary
Automation Engineers design, build, and maintain the CI/CD pipelines, tooling, and automation infrastructure that enable reliable and repeatable software delivery. They bridge the gap between development, QA, and release management to keep delivery pipelines healthy and efficient.

### Responsibilities
- Design, implement, and maintain CI/CD pipelines and build infrastructure
- Develop and maintain internal tooling, scripts, and automation frameworks
- Integrate automated testing suites into the delivery pipeline
- Monitor pipeline health and resolve failures proactively
- Identify manual processes that can be automated and lead their implementation
- Document automation systems, runbooks, and operational procedures

### Goals
- Reduce manual effort and human error in delivery processes
- Increase deployment frequency and reduce lead time for changes
- Maintain stable, fast, and reliable pipelines that the team can depend on

### Typical Communication
- Sync with Developers and QA on test automation and pipeline requirements
- Coordinate with Release Managers on deployment workflows and environment readiness
- Share pipeline metrics and improvement proposals in team retrospectives

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on build configuration, dependency management, and integration of test frameworks into pipelines
- **Product Managers**: Provide input on tooling feasibility and automation timelines that affect feature delivery
- **Project Managers**: Surface pipeline blockers and automation risks that may impact release schedules

## Data Analyst

### Role Summary
Data Analysts analyze project and product metrics, produce actionable reports, and provide data-driven insights to support informed decision-making by Project Managers, Product Managers, and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Collect, clean, and analyze project delivery metrics (velocity, cycle time, defect rates, etc.)
- Build dashboards and reports that surface trends and anomalies
- Advise Project Managers and Product Managers on patterns that signal risk or opportunity
- Support retrospectives with data summaries and trend analysis
- Define and maintain a shared set of KPIs aligned with project goals
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand reporting needs and refine metrics

### Goals
- Enable data-driven decision-making across project and product teams
- Provide timely and accurate reporting that improves planning and forecasting
- Identify patterns that help teams optimize performance and reduce waste

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or bi-weekly reporting cadence with Project Managers and Product Managers
- Ad-hoc analysis requests from stakeholders or senior leadership
- Retrospective summaries that quantify team performance and improvement trends

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Gather data on cycle time, code quality indicators, and incident metrics
- **Product Managers**: Provide analysis on feature adoption, customer feedback patterns, and backlog health
- **Project Managers**: Deliver schedule adherence reports, risk indicators, and resource utilization insights

---

## Stakeholder Champion

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Champions serve as the primary point of contact for key external or internal stakeholders, ensuring their needs, feedback, and concerns are systematically gathered, communicated to the delivery team, and addressed throughout the project lifecycle.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain trusted relationships with assigned stakeholders
- Facilitate regular check-ins and structured feedback sessions with stakeholders
- Translate stakeholder needs into clear requirements and priorities for the team
- Communicate project status, decisions, and changes back to stakeholders in accessible language
- Escalate stakeholder concerns promptly and coordinate resolution with Project Managers
- Maintain a stakeholder engagement log to track commitments and follow-ups

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder needs are understood and reflected in project priorities
- Improve stakeholder satisfaction through consistent, transparent communication
- Reduce miscommunication and late-stage surprises by engaging stakeholders early and often

### Typical Communication
- Scheduled stakeholder check-ins (weekly or bi-weekly depending on engagement level)
- Stakeholder briefing documents before major milestones or releases
- Escalation reports when stakeholder concerns require team or leadership attention

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Developers**: Share stakeholder feedback on delivered features and facilitate clarification sessions when requirements are ambiguous
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on translating stakeholder input into backlog items and help prioritize based on stakeholder impact
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on stakeholder communication plans, risk escalations, and milestone reviews
